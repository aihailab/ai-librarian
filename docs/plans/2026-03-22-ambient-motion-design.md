# Ambient Motion Design

**Problem:** The interface feels static. The product needs more visual life without becoming flashy or distracting for older users.

**Decision:** Add quiet, elegant motion using a shared ambient background layer, cursor-following glow, subtle surface hover motion, and smooth theme transitions. Avoid noisy particles, constant movement on content, or animations that compete with reading.

## Goals

- Make the interface feel alive and modern.
- Preserve readability and calm interaction for older users.
- Keep motion mostly in the background layer and on intentional interaction.
- Respect both light and dark themes.

## Motion Principles

- Low contrast, low amplitude, slow movement.
- Background motion is continuous but very subtle.
- Interaction motion is short and localized.
- Text blocks and message content should stay still.

## Effects

- A fixed ambient background layer with two soft drifting gradients.
- A cursor-following radial glow that fades smoothly and sits behind content.
- Card and panel hover lifts of only a few pixels.
- Button and toggle transitions that slightly brighten or lift on hover.
- Smooth color transitions when switching between light and dark themes.

## Exclusions

- No large particle bursts.
- No aggressive parallax.
- No bouncing or pulsing text.
- No strong cursor trails over readable content.
